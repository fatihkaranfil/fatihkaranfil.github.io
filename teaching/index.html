03 / TEACHING

Teaching economics through theory, evidence, and application.

My teaching connects analytical foundations with empirical methods and real-world economic policy questions.

TEACHING APPROACH

From concepts to policy problems.

I emphasize clear economic reasoning, quantitative literacy, and the ability to interpret evidence critically. Students are encouraged to move from models and data to practical questions in energy, environment, and economic development.
