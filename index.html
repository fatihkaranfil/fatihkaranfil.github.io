ECONOMIST | ENERGY, ENVIRONMENT & PUBLIC POLICY

Advancing research in energy economics
Informing policy decisions

I am Fatih Karanfil, Principal Fellow at KAPSARC in Riyadh. My research lies at the intersection of energy economics, environmental economics, and public policy, with a particular focus on energy transitions, industrial development, and economic diversification. I combine economic theory, quantitative methods, and empirical analysis to address policy challenges, bridging academic research and real-world decision-making. My current work aims to provide evidence-based insights into the economic and structural transformations shaping Saudi Arabia and oil-exporting economies more broadly.

01 / RESEARCH

Areas of interest

01

Energy economics & policy

Economic analysis of resource allocation, investment decisions, and the opportunity cost of oil, with a focus on the implications of energy transitions for economic development and structural transformation.

02

Macroeconomics of resource-rich economies

Fiscal policy, economic diversification, discount rates for public investment opportunities, labor markets, and structural adjustment in oil-exporting countries.

03

Climate & environmental economics

Carbon pricing, emissions trading, renewable energy, and the economic design of environmental policy.

02 / POLICY IMPACT

From economic research to policy decisions

Alongside my academic research, I contribute to KAPSARC's advisory activities, providing subject-matter expertise and economic analysis on strategic investment projects and policy questions relevant to Saudi Arabia.

My work focusses on economic impact analysis of investment strategies, energy policy, fiscal policy, and economic diversification. Combining economic modeling, empirical research, and sectoral expertise, I contribute developing economic tools and providing insights that inform policy formulation, support strategic decision-making, and contribute to long-term economic development and value creation in the Kingdom.

These contributions form part of KAPSARC's broader advisory mission, supporting key government stakeholders, including the Ministry of Energy and the Ministry of Industry and Mineral Resources.

04 / ABOUT

Research with a policy perspective

Since 2017, I have worked at KAPSARC, contributing research and advisory input on energy and economic policy. Previously, I taught economics at the University of Paris Nanterre and conducted research at EconomiX-CNRS.

I hold a Ph.D. in Economics from Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne / Paris School of Economics and a Habilitation à Diriger des Recherches (HDR), the French accreditation to supervise doctoral research.
