ECONOMIST · ENERGY & ENVIRONMENT

Researching energy markets.
Informing economic policy.

I am Fatih Karanfil, Principal Fellow at KAPSARC in Riyadh. My work connects rigorous economic research with policy analysis in energy, industrial development, and economic diversification. I contribute to policy-oriented work relevant to Saudi Arabia’s Ministry of Energy and Ministry of Industry and Mineral Resources.

04 / ABOUT

Research with a policy perspective.

Since 2017, I have worked at KAPSARC, contributing research and advisory input on energy and economic policy. Previously, I taught economics at the University of Paris Nanterre and conducted research at EconomiX-CNRS.

I hold a Ph.D. in Economics from Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne / Paris School of Economics and an HDR, the French accreditation to supervise research.
