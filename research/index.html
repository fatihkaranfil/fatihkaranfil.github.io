01 / RESEARCH

Economic research for relevant policy questions.

My current research combines economic theory and quantitative methods to examine the challenges and opportunities associated with structural transformation of resource-rich economies.

RESEARCH THEMES

Three connected areas of inquiry.

01

Energy economics & policy

Resource allocation, oil-market fundamentals, energy investment, and the opportunity cost of oil in producer economies.

02

Climate & environmental economics

Carbon pricing, emissions trading, renewable energy deployment, critical raw materials, and climate policy evaluation.

03

Macroeconomics of resource-rich economies

Fiscal policy, labor markets, economic diversification, and structural adjustment in oil- and gas-exporting countries.

CURRENT RESEARCH

Selected research directions.

Energy transition

Renewables, critical raw materials, and import dependence

Investigating how the expansion of renewable energy changes external dependencies and creates new vulnerabilities.

Carbon markets

Emissions allowance allocation and reinvestment

Assessing how auctioning and free allocation of carbon allowances interact with investment incentives and renewable energy deployment.

Resource economies

Foreign labor and structural adjustment in the GCC

Studying the role of migration in the transmission of oil-price shocks to tradable and non-tradable sectors.

Connecting rigorous analysis with policy needs.
